The pipeline

01

Drop

Give it a PNG spritesheet and its matching Sparrow/Starling XML atlas.

02

Scan

Every SubTexture is measured pixel-by-pixel for transparent padding beyond its declared frame.

03

Repack

Trimmed frames are re-packed into the smallest sheet that fits, edges extruded to stop bleeding.

04

Download

Optimized PNG, rewritten XML and a savings report land in a .zip, downloaded automatically.
